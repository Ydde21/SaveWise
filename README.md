🎯 Purpose

SaveWise answers a simple but powerful question:

“When will I reach my financial goal?”

Instead of just tracking money, the app analyzes behavior, debt impact, and savings capacity to project realistic outcomes.

🧠 Financial Logic

SaveWise follows real financial behavior:

Interest is annualized but compounded (like digital banks)

Credit card purchase = expense

Credit card payment = debt payment (no double counting)

Net Flow = Income − Expenses − Loan Payments

This prevents misleading projections common in simple trackers.
